# Archived results: January to May 2024

These are the reports as they stood when the data covered January to May 2024 (yellow taxis
only), kept unchanged so the effect of extending the data to a full year can be compared. They
are **not** the current results; the current ones are in the parent directory. They are byte-identical
to the files committed at `50d02dd` (the last commit before the data was extended), generated by the commands
in [EVALUATION](../../docs/EVALUATION.md).

The two sets of results are not directly comparable: the test window moved from April 6 to May 31
(ordinary days) to November 6 to December 31 (which contains Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year),
and the training history is twelve months instead of five.
