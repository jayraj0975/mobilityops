# Data sources, attribution and terms

MobilityOps uses only public data. **No raw trip files are stored in, or redistributed by, this
repository.** The pipeline downloads them from their owners at run time.

| Data | Publisher | Used for | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yellow Taxi Trip Records (Parquet, monthly) | NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission (TLC) | trips, fares, zones, timestamps | https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/about/tlc-trip-record-data.page |
| Green Taxi and High Volume For-Hire Vehicle Trip Records (Parquet, monthly), optional | NYC TLC | pickup counts per zone-hour for green taxis and ride-hail services | same page |
| Taxi Zone Lookup table | NYC TLC | zone names, boroughs | same page |
| Taxi Zones geometry | NYC Open Data (TLC) | zone centroids for distances | https://data.cityofnewyork.us/d/8meu-9t5y |
| Daily weather, station USW00094728 (Central Park) | NOAA NCEI, GHCN-Daily | rain, snow, temperature context | https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/ |

The TLC notes that trip records are provided by taxi technology providers and that the TLC does
not guarantee their accuracy or completeness; this project's cleaning rules (see
`docs/DATA_DICTIONARY.md`) exist because of that. This project is **not affiliated with or endorsed
by the NYC TLC, the City of New York or NOAA.** Consult the publishers' pages for their current
terms of use before reusing their data.

## What this project publishes

* **Code** under the MIT license (`LICENSE`).
* **Derived aggregates only** in `reports/` and in the hosted demo's data snapshot: counts of taxi
  pickups per zone-hour, forecasts, anomaly events and evaluation metrics computed from the source
  data. They contain no individual trips, drivers or passengers. The zone-hour tables do include
  `revenue`, a per-zone-hour sum of the trips' total fare, but no individual payment record. (The
  TLC source records themselves carry fare components and a payment-type code for each trip, and, per
  the TLC's published data dictionary, no names, card numbers or other direct identifiers.)
* **Synthetic data** generated by `mobilityops.sample`, always labelled `TEST / SYNTHETIC DATA`.

Results describe yellow-taxi pickups in New York for the stated months, unless a result says it
also covers green taxis and high-volume for-hire vehicles. They are not statements about other
years, other cities, or the older non-high-volume for-hire files, which are not used.
